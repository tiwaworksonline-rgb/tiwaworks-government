06 / Frequently asked questions

Common procurement questions.

Clear information for agencies, purchasing departments and prospective teaming partners.

Does TiwaWorks bid as a prime contractor?

TiwaWorks is positioning its government contracting operations around prime contract management, subject to eligibility, resources and the terms of each solicitation.

Can TiwaWorks coordinate licensed trade services?

Where permitted, appropriately licensed and qualified subcontractors may perform regulated trade work. Contract-specific qualifications, oversight and subcontracting restrictions must be reviewed before bidding.

Where can buyers find registration identifiers?

Verified SAM.gov registration details, UEI, CAGE and NAICS codes will be published when confirmed. We do not display unverified identifiers.

How can suppliers become partners?

Prospective suppliers can initiate a conversation through the main TiwaWorks website. Qualification and onboarding procedures are being developed.

Are the photographs actual TiwaWorks projects?

No. Images on this preview are illustrative and do not establish past performance. Verified project photography may be added separately.
