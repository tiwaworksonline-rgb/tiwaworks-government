About TiwaWorks, Inc.

Experience in execution. Built for what's next.

TiwaWorks brings together event production, communications, project coordination and supplier relationships to help organizations turn complex requirements into organized, accountable delivery.

Who we are

A multidisciplinary company with a delivery-first mindset.

TiwaWorks, Inc. is a business services and project coordination company with experience spanning live events, experiential marketing, communications and production. Our approach centers on planning, people, decades of professional relationships and the operational details that help projects move from concept to completion.

For government contracting, we organize our capabilities around the needs of public agencies: clear scopes of work, responsive supplier coordination, dependable communication and disciplined project oversight.

We believe the strongest projects start with a clear understanding of the client's objectives and a team aligned to deliver them.
